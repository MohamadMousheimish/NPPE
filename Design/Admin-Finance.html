CRA · GST/HST

The CA$30,000 threshold

You're a “small supplier” — no need to register for or charge GST/HST — until your taxable revenue passes CA$30,000, measured either in a single calendar quarter or over the last four consecutive quarters (rolling). This gauge tracks that rolling total so nothing sneaks up on you.

If you cross CA$30,000 without registering, you can owe the tax out of pocket, retroactively — so we flag orange at CA$20k and red at CA$25k to give you runway.

When it turns red, here's the play:

  • Register with the CRA for a Business Number + GST/HST account (free, online). Add QST separately if you sell into Quebec.
  • Charge the customer's provincial rate — 5% GST in most of the West, 13% HST in Ontario, 14–15% in the Atlantic provinces. Sales outside Canada are zero-rated.
  • Decide pricing — add tax on top of CA$29 / CA$9.99, or treat the price as tax-inclusive.
  • Turn on Stripe Tax so the right rate is applied automatically by province (one small config change), then file & remit on the CRA's schedule.

Not tax advice — confirm timing and rates with a CPA and the CRA. Sources: canada.ca (when to register) · docs.stripe.com/tax
