QUESTION 01

An engineer discovers a design flaw posing a public-safety risk. First obligation?

B Your answer — Take steps to safeguard the public, including notifying the appropriate authority.
Correct. An engineer's paramount duty is to public welfare. That duty comes before contractual or employer obligations, so safeguarding the public is always the first step.
QUESTION 12

Which limitation period applies to a claim in professional negligence?

A Your answer — A fixed two years from the date the work was completed.
C Correct answer — Generally two years from when the claim was, or ought to have been, discovered.
Review. Limitation periods typically run from discoverability, not simply from completion of the work — the "discoverability principle." The distinction is a common NPPE trap.
QUESTION 18

An engineer may accept a gift from a supplier when:

D Your answer — Any time, provided the work is unaffected.
B Correct answer — Only when it is nominal and could not reasonably be seen to influence judgment.
Review. The test is not just actual influence but the appearance of influence. Nominal, transparent courtesies are acceptable; anything that could reasonably be seen to sway judgment is not.
